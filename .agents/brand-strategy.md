## 01 — WHAT IS KORRIDO?

Korrido was born from a simple observation: the richest advertising real estate in any city is hurtling through it right now, carrying a captive audience for twenty to thirty uninterrupted minutes — and nobody is using it well. Aravind S — an AI-native engineering leader who built and scaled software teams at Anthriq and shipped AI compliance products at RADICALi — saw the gap between measurable digital advertising and the captive, premium attention of a cab ride. He partnered with Vishal Prathush R, a product manager with fintech and compliance depth from Stripe, PwC, and Amazon, who had already co-founded ventures in adtech and creator tech. Together they built the platform that turns every cab ride into measurable, targeted, premium advertising space.

Korrido installs HD tablet screens inside commercial cabs and broadcasts digital advertising campaigns to passengers during their rides. Advertisers upload creatives through a self-serve platform, select their target neighborhoods and time windows, and go live across the fleet within 48 hours — no printing, no installation crews, no agency middleman. GPS movement data powers impression tracking and zone-based targeting, so brands know exactly where and when their message played. Drivers earn passive monthly income. Passengers get a screen that carries useful local content alongside ads. And the whole system runs without a single camera, audio sensor, or biometric — a line Korrido refuses to cross.

## 04 — BRAND VALUES

**Innovation**
Korrido treats technology as the product, not the backend. From wireless creative propagation to GPS-based impression modeling, every system is built to make the impossible look effortless. The team ships fast and iterates faster.

**Transparency**
Every advertiser sees the same data Korrido sees — impressions, vehicle uptime, zone reach. No vanity metrics, no inflation, no hidden multipliers. Drivers receive clear weekly statements. Passengers are never tracked. Honesty is the brand's sharpest differentiator.

**Local-First**
Korrido hires locally, partners with Indian cab fleets, and prioritizes local advertisers. The cab in front of you is run by a real person from your city. Every zone is mapped by people who drive it, not algorithms that guess it.

**Premium Quality**
The screens are HD. The creatives are well-formatted. The dashboard is clean. The onboarding is 48 hours. Every touchpoint is designed to feel like a product, not a service — because premium placement demands a premium experience.

**Measurable Impact**
If it can't be measured, it doesn't belong on the platform. Korrido replaces the guesswork of outdoor advertising with live dashboards, play-proof logs, and GPS-verified zone coverage. Brands don't buy "exposure" — they buy verified impressions.

**Quality · Real · Care · Honest · Verified**

## 05 — BRAND GOALS

**Lock Down South Bengaluru:** Establish Korrido as the dominant in-cab advertising network across high-net-worth concentration zones in South Bengaluru — Koramangala, HSR Layout, Indiranagar, Whitefield, Jayanagar, JP Nagar — before expanding outward. 100+ cabs live today; target 500+ by end of next year.

**Scale to Six States:** Expand from Karnataka into Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, and Delhi NCR. Each city launches with the same hyper-local zone mapping that made Bengaluru work — no copy-paste, no shortcuts.

**Build the Self-Serve Standard:** Make Korrido's platform as familiar as Meta Ads — same mental model, same speed, same ease. An advertiser should be able to upload, target, and go live without ever speaking to a salesperson.

**Earn Driver Loyalty:** Drivers are not inventory — they are partners. Transparent weekly payouts, zero passenger tracking, and no extra work create a relationship drivers don't want to leave. Driver retention is a growth metric, not a cost center.

**Establish the Trust Benchmark:** Make "zero cameras, zero biometrics" the industry standard. Korrido's pledge — honest measurement, fair economics, locally rooted operations — should become the baseline that competitors are measured against, not a feature that needs explaining.

**Hit Revenue Sustainability:** Reach a run rate where fleet expansion and advertiser acquisition fund each other without external dependency. Launch pricing closes soon; the transition to standard pricing is the inflection point.

## 08 — BRAND VOICE

*Korrido speaks with confident clarity — direct, premium, and quietly bold.*

The voice lives in the territory between a sharp product launch and an honest conversation. It never oversells. It never name-drops competitors. It makes claims it can back with a dashboard. When Korrido says "live in 48 hours," it means 48 hours. When it says "zero cameras," it means zero cameras. The voice is at its best when it is making a promise and then immediately showing the receipt.

**Direct**
Korrido doesn't hedge. Sentences are short. Claims are specific. "20–30 minutes of attention per ride. No scroll-skip. No ad-blocker." The brand says what it does and moves on.

**Premium**
The language is clean, not flashy. No exclamation marks. No hype words. The premium feel comes from precision — the right word, the right number, the right zone — not from adjectives.

**Confident**
Korrido states its position without comparison. "We're taking over India's streets" is a statement of momentum, not a jab at anyone else. The confidence comes from the product, not from positioning against others.

**Local**
Korrido names neighborhoods like a local. Koramangala, HSR Layout, Indiranagar — not "South Bengaluru" generically. The brand knows its zones the way a driver knows its routes.

**Honest**
When Korrido makes a pledge, it sounds like a pledge. "Zero cameras, zero biometrics — ever." The word "ever" does the heavy lifting. The voice doesn't qualify, caveat, or fine-print its promises.

**Energetic**
There is momentum in the language. "Filling." "Live now." "Still counting." The brand is building something and it wants you to feel the pace — without sounding desperate or salesy.

**Plain-Spoken**
No jargon. "Familiar like Meta Ads. Captive like cinema. Local like a hoarding — but live in minutes." Complex technology is explained in analogies a cab driver would nod at.

## 09 — COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

*Note: Korrido never names, compares against, or references competitors in public-facing communications. This analysis is for internal strategic use only.*

### In-Cab / Mobility Advertising Platforms (India)

**Generic In-Cab Screen Networks**
- **Overview:** Several operators run digital screens inside cabs, autos, and buses across Indian cities, typically with broad city-wide coverage and no zone-level targeting.
- **Strengths:** Existing fleet relationships. Volume reach across mass transit.
- **Weaknesses:** Broad, untargeted coverage with no neighborhood-level precision. Limited measurement — often no real-time dashboard. Slow creative deployment (days to weeks). No transparent impression methodology.
- **How Korrido is different:** Korrido targets high-net-worth concentration zones specifically, not the whole city. Campaigns go live in 48 hours with GPS-verified zone tracking and a live dashboard. The measurement standard is higher, the deployment is faster, and the audience is premium by design.

**Traditional OOH / Hoarding Operators (Going Digital)**
- **Overview:** Legacy outdoor advertising companies adding digital billboards and screens to their inventory, typically at fixed locations.
- **Strengths:** Established advertiser relationships. Large existing inventory. Brand recognition.
- **Weaknesses:** Static placement — no mobility, no zone-level targeting within a city. Long lead times for creative changes. No impression-level measurement. Premium placements are expensive and limited.
- **How Korrido is different:** Korrido's screens move through the city, reaching passengers inside a captive environment — not passersby who may or may not look up. Creative changes propagate in minutes, not weeks. And the cost of reaching a premium audience in a specific zone is a fraction of a single hoarding rental.

### Digital Advertising Platforms (Indirect Competitors)

**Meta Ads (Facebook / Instagram)**
- **Overview:** The default self-serve advertising platform for Indian businesses of all sizes.
- **Strengths:** Massive reach. Familiar self-serve interface. Granular audience targeting. Instant deployment.
- **Weaknesses:** Rising costs. Ad fatigue. Scroll-skip behavior. No captive attention. No physical-world context. No premium placement feel.
- **How Korrido is different:** Korrido borrows the self-serve mental model — "upload, set zone, go live" — but places the ad inside a 20–30 minute captive ride where there is no scroll, no skip, and no ad-blocker. The audience is premium by zone selection, not by algorithmic guess.

**Google Ads (Search / Display / YouTube)**
- **Overview:** Intent-based and display advertising across Google's network.
- **Strengths:** Intent capture on search. Massive display network. Measurable.
- **Weaknesses:** Display has low attention. YouTube is skippable. No physical-world presence. No captive context.
- **How Korrido is different:** Korrido is not intent-based — it is context-based. The passenger didn't search for anything. They are sitting in a cab, looking at a screen, for 20–30 minutes. That is a different kind of attention, and it commands a different kind of recall.

## 10 — POSITIONING

### Positioning Narrative

The outdoor advertising industry in India has been stuck in a trade-off: you can have reach (hoardings, mass transit screens) but not targeting or measurement, or you can have targeting and measurement (Meta, Google) but not captive, premium, physical-world attention. The gap between these two worlds is where Korrido sits. It takes the self-serve ease and dashboard accountability of digital advertising and applies them to the most underutilized premium surface in the city — the screen inside a cab carrying an affluent commuter through a high-net-worth neighborhood for 20–30 uninterrupted minutes. No existing player combines all three: hyper-local zone targeting, captive attention, and real-time measurement. Korrido does, and it refuses to compromise on any of the three.

### Positioning Statement

Korrido is the hyper-local in-cab advertising network that brings the measurability and speed of digital advertising to the captive, premium attention of a cab ride — live in 48 hours, tracked by GPS, targeted by neighborhood.

### Positioning Map

**X-Axis:** Broad / Mass Reach ← → Hyper-Local / Zone-Targeted
**Y-Axis:** Unmeasured / Slow ← → Measured / Real-Time

- **Traditional OOH:** Broad reach, unmeasured, slow (bottom-left)
- **Mass in-cab screen networks:** Broad reach, limited measurement (bottom-center)
- **Meta / Google Ads:** Targeted, measured, but no captive context (top-right, lower on attention)
- **Korrido:** Hyper-local, measured, captive, real-time (top-right, highest on both axes)

Korrido occupies the top-right quadrant alone — the only player that is both hyper-local and fully measured while offering captive, premium attention.

## 11 — HOW KORRIDO STANDS OUT

> *"Familiar like Meta Ads. Captive like cinema. Local like a hoarding — but live in minutes."*

Korrido refuses to be unmeasured. It refuses to be slow. It refuses to track passengers. It refuses to treat drivers as inventory. Every one of these refusals closes a door that competitors leave open — and each one is a door that advertisers, drivers, and passengers care about. The brand commits instead to speed (48 hours to live), precision (zone-level GPS targeting), honesty (zero cameras, transparent dashboards), and partnership (fair driver economics). These aren't features. They are the terms on which Korrido operates, and they are non-negotiable. In a market where most players compete on volume and price, Korrido competes on trust, speed, and premium placement — and that combination is structurally difficult to copy.

A functional tagline that pairs the two things advertisers care about most: placement and proof. It is descriptive, not emotional, but it is exactly the promise the category has been missing.

Minimal and confident. Positions Korrido as a medium, not a service — the way "the billboard" or "the feed" describes a channel. Best used in brand-level communications, not performance campaigns.

A values-forward tagline that leads with the two things that separate Korrido from every alternative. It is a quiet flex — no superlatives, no comparisons, just the terms.
