# Brand Context

## Brand
- **Name**: Korrido
- **Category**: In-cab digital advertising / mobility media
- **Description**: Digital in-cab advertising on tablet screens inside city cabs, reaching commuters during their 20–30 minute ride with live impression tracking.
- **Stage**: Early — deployed in 100+ cabs across South Bengaluru, targeting high-net-worth concentration areas.
- **Website**: https://korrido.com

## Audience
- **Primary Audience**: Brands and businesses wanting to reach affluent commuters in high-net-worth concentration areas of South Bengaluru through captive in-cab digital screens.
- **Key Problem**: Traditional outdoor advertising (hoardings) is static and untargeted; digital ads (Meta/Google) lack the captive, high-attention context of a 20–30 minute cab ride. Brands struggle to reach high-net-worth individuals in a premium, distraction-free environment with measurable impact.
- **Their Language**: "hyper-local targeting", "captive audience", "live impression tracking", "premium environment", "measurable ROI", "high net worth areas"

## Positioning
- **Differentiation**: Korrido's edge is its concentrated deployment in high-net-worth areas of South Bengaluru — 100+ cabs live on the ground, reaching affluent commuters where they spend time. The platform combines the familiarity of digital ad platforms (like Meta Ads) with the captive attention of cinema advertising and the hyper-local relevance of outdoor hoardings — but live in minutes.
- **Competitors**: Other in-cab / mobility advertising platforms operating in Indian cities. (Brand never compares itself directly with any competitor in public-facing communications.)
- **Market Position**: Niche premium — focused on high-net-worth concentration areas with measurable, location-aware advertising.

## Brand Personality
- **Personality Words**: Bold, Premium, Local, Data-driven, Trustworthy
- **Tone**: Confident and direct. Professional but not stiff. Energetic about growth and expansion. Never comparative — speaks only about its own strengths and momentum.
- **Voice Admires**: None specified

## Values & Mission
- **Core Values**: Innovation, Transparency, Local-first, Premium quality, Measurable impact
- **Mission**: To bridge the gap between outdoor advertising and digital marketing by offering measurable, data-driven, location-aware advertising solutions. Every journey becomes an opportunity for brands to reach thousands of highly engaged passengers in a premium, distraction-free environment.

## Goals
- **Primary Goal**: Scale from 100+ cabs in South Bengaluru to a multi-city presence across India (Karnataka → Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Kerala, Delhi NCR).
- **Key Metrics**: Cab count deployed, active advertiser campaigns, monthly impressions, city expansion milestones, advertiser retention rate.

## Competitive Edge (Internal — Never Used in Public Communications)
- Korrido has a concentrated deployment advantage in high-net-worth areas of South Bengaluru (100+ cabs live).
- The brand never references, compares against, or mentions any competitor by name in any public-facing content, copy, or marketing material.
- All messaging focuses on Korrido's own strengths, momentum, and value proposition.
